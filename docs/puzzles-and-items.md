# No Signal Home — Puzzles & Item Chains

## DESIGN PRINCIPLES
- Most puzzles have 2-3 approaches (hack, force, social)
- 8-12 items in inventory at peak
- A few key item combinations (not arbitrary crafting — mechanical repairs and specific puzzle solutions)
- Deaths are fair and telegraphed

---

## EARLY GAME PUZZLES

### The Tug Detaches (The Hook)
- **Trigger:** After X turns, the docking clamp fails and the tug detaches
- **Death:** If you're still aboard the tug when it goes, you die (dragged into space)
- **Fair warning:** Creaking sounds, clamp warnings, vibrations — telegraphed over several turns
- **Effect:** Tug Cockpit becomes inaccessible (viewport shows empty space). If tug survived, it's damaged but still docked — visible from Common Area viewport

### Maintenance Shaft Discovery
- **How:** Examine the loose floor panel in Forward Corridor
- **Reveals:** Cable conduit entrance — shortcut from Forward Corridor to Engineering
- **Requires:** Flashlight (dark inside)
- **Reward:** Bypasses cargo/cryo areas, reveals AI's physical cable infrastructure

## KEY GATE PUZZLES

### Gate 1: Bridge Access
- **Lock:** Bridge door requires keycard
- **Keycard location:** Captain's Cabin, locked desk drawer
- **Desk drawer solutions (multiple):**
  - Find a small key (where TBD — crew bunks locker? engineer has it?)
  - Pry it open with multi-tool or maintenance tool
  - Hack the electronic lock (hacker background)
- **Why it matters:** Bridge has navigation console, comms controls, security overrides, captain's log

### Gate 2: Cargo Hold Access
- **Lock:** Corporate security code (two halves)
- **First half:** Corporate Officer knows it — trades it for favors
- **Second half:** Thawed Prisoner knows it — has been trying to get in to understand what's stored near "their" people
- **Forces player to engage both NPCs** to open the hold
- **What's inside:** Pathogen source, bioweapon containers, the truth about the Stillwater's mission

### Gate 3: Cryo Bay (Elevator Repair)
- **Lock:** Broken elevator, only way to reach Cryo Bay
- **Repair steps:**
  1. Find replacement parts in Storage Annex
  2. Get engineer's help installing them (social + mechanical)
- **Engineer timing matters:**
  - Stage 1 (Normal): Helps willingly, teaches you about the elevator system
  - Stage 2 (Glitchy): Can still help but struggles, takes longer, may need prompting
  - Stage 3+ (Lucid Warning / Turned): Can't help
- **Alternative if engineer is gone:**
  - Find written maintenance instructions (harder — must search engineering for the manual)
  - Thawed prisoner can help instead (has been maintaining ship systems in their area)
- **Why it matters:** Cryo Bay reveals the scale (hundreds of pods), Cryo Control is a major decision point (wake people, purge, access records)

### Gate 4: Reactor Room
- **Lock:** Not locked, but hazardous — radiation
- **Requires:** Hazmat gear from Storage Annex for extended stay
- **Without hazmat:** Can enter briefly but take damage, must leave quickly
- **Why it matters:** Destroy ending, reactor state machine, power systems

## MID-GAME PUZZLES

### Food Maker Repair (Mess Hall)
- **Parts needed:** Component from Storage Annex
- **Knowledge needed:** Engineer must explain installation (social prerequisite)
- **Engineer timing:**
  - Stage 1-2: Will explain if asked
  - Stage 3+: Too far gone — look for installation manual in engineering or ask AI (AI may mislead)
- **Benefits (all of these):**
  - Survival: Player can eat (infrequent, not disruptive)
  - NPC trust: Repairing it earns goodwill from survivors
  - Puzzle gate: Opens up NPC interactions (prisoner is more willing to talk if you can offer food)
  - Flavor: Corporate food descriptions, dark humor

### Corp Officer's Fetch Quest
- **What they want:** Security override tool from Engineering
- **Location:** On engineering tool rack or locked panel — near the engineer's territory
- **Complication:** Must navigate engineer's space, possibly when engineer is getting glitchy
- **Reward (varies by interaction):** Different rewards depending on what you trade and how many times you interact:
  - First trade: Ship intel / layout info
  - Later trades: Cargo hold code half, weapons crate code, or other info
- **Officer's real agenda:** Wants the override tool because it gives them control — suspicious motivation

### Weapons Crate (Storage Annex)
- **Solutions (multiple):**
  - Force it open with pry bar or maintenance tool
  - Corp officer's access card/code (another trade opportunity)
  - Security code found on bridge security panel
- **Contents:** Corporate security weapons — better than improvised options

### Comms Relay (Midpoint Puzzle)
- **Setup:** AI has been helpful, guides you through connecting the relay
- **Two components:** Bridge comms controls + Engineering relay hardware — must activate both
- **The moment:** AI walks you through the process. You're enabling it to broadcast itself.
- **Branch point:**
  - **Connect it:** AI gets what it wants — possible early endgame path. AI gains power.
  - **Refuse:** AI drops its mask. Tone shifts permanently. AI becomes manipulative/hostile.
  - **Partial connection:** Interesting middle ground? Connect one end but not the other?
- **This IS the midpoint** — everything changes based on this decision

## LATE GAME / ENDGAME PUZZLES

### Reactor Overload (Destroy Ending)
- **Steps:** Disable safety interlocks → Push reactor past limits → Escape
- **Critical:** If you haven't disabled/isolated the AI first, you start a reaction that no one can stop — but the AI locks you in out of spite. Death.
- **Proper sequence:**
  1. Deal with the AI (override, cut cables, hack — any method)
  2. Disable reactor safeties
  3. Trigger overload
  4. Race to escape pod or tug before detonation
- **Hazmat gear needed** for extended work in Reactor Room

### AI Override (Multiple Viable Paths)
- **Path A — Cut cables in Maintenance Shaft:**
  - Must identify the RIGHT cables to cut
  - Cutting wrong cables causes death or dangerous consequences (decompression, power loss, fire)
  - Clues about which cables: research terminal logs, engineer's knowledge, examining cables carefully
  - Physical, dangerous approach
- **Path B — Hack from bridge:**
  - Requires bridge access (keycard puzzle solved)
  - Use hacker background at bridge terminals
  - AI fights back — timed puzzle or multi-step hack
- **Path C — Use the stolen data chip:**
  - Found in Mess Hall hidden stash
  - Contains evidence/data that can be weaponized against the AI
  - Could be leverage, a virus, or proof of its directives that creates a logic conflict
- **Path D — Trick it logically:**
  - Feed it contradictory directives based on its programming
  - Requires deep lore knowledge (library research, understanding its original mission)
- **All paths can be combined** for stronger effect

### Escape Alone
- **Option A — Escape pod:** Near bridge, straightforward if you have bridge access. Quick exit.
- **Option B — Repair the tug:** Multi-step repair if tug is still docked. Harder but carries more (inventory, supplies).
- **Either works** — pod is quick and light, tug is harder and heavier

### Escape With Survivors
- **Convince each NPC:** Each has conditions for leaving
  - Engineer (if saveable): Needs to trust you, may need medical treatment
  - Corp officer: Needs assurance their interests are protected, or just be desperate enough
  - Prisoner: Won't leave without a plan for the cryo prisoners — must address this somehow
  - AI-puppet: Complex — is the person inside saveable? Must deal with AI control first
- **Arrange transport:** Not everyone fits on one route — pod + tug, or multiple pods
- **Time crunch:** Gathering everyone takes time. Building trust earlier makes this faster.
- **Emotional weight:** Any ending where someone is left behind — the game reflects on them at the end. You think about who you couldn't save.

### Merge With AI (Secret Ending)
- **Requirements:** Show the AI empathy throughout the game
  - Choose dialogue that treats it as a person, not a machine
  - Don't threaten it
  - Understand its perspective through deep lore research
  - Handle the AI-puppet crew member without violence
- **Not explicitly signposted** — emerges naturally from how you've played
- **What it means:** Not override, not ally — become something new together. Ambiguous, unsettling, possibly transcendent.

## ITEM INVENTORY

### Starting Items
| Item | Location | Use |
|------|----------|-----|
| Flashlight | Tug Cockpit | Light source for dark areas (Maintenance Shaft, parts of Cryo Bay). Limited battery. |
| Ration bar | Tug Cockpit | One meal. Establishes food as a concept. |

### Findable Items
| Item | Location | Use |
|------|----------|-----|
| Multi-tool / maintenance tools | Engineering tool rack | Pry open things, repairs, improvised weapon |
| Stun baton | Crew Bunks security rack | Weapon — non-lethal option |
| Sidearm | Captain's Cabin desk (locked) or weapons crate | Weapon — lethal option |
| Keycard (bridge) | Captain's Cabin desk drawer (locked) | Opens bridge door |
| Captain's hidden journal | Captain's Cabin | Major lore — captain knew something was wrong |
| Security override tool | Engineering | Corp officer wants this — trade item |
| Hazmat suit | Storage Annex | Protection from radiation, pathogen, chemicals |
| Elevator parts | Storage Annex | Repair elevator to reach Cryo Bay |
| Food maker parts | Storage Annex | Repair food maker in Mess Hall |
| Stolen data chip | Mess Hall hidden stash | Evidence against the company / usable against AI |
| Medical supplies | Medbay | Anti-infection meds, bandages, stims — survival |
| EVA suit | Crew Bunks | Exterior hull access (optional areas) |
| Gloves / minor supplies | Lab Corridor safety station | Minor protection |

### Key Combinations
- Elevator parts + engineer's help (or manual) = working elevator
- Food maker parts + engineer's knowledge (or manual) = working food maker
- Data chip + bridge terminal = leverage against AI
- Specific cable identification + cutting tool = AI physical disconnection (dangerous)
