# No Signal Home — Room Map

## THE STILLWATER (25 rooms)

### Vertical Connections
- **Ladder:** Amidships — connects Lower Mid Corridor ↔ Mid Central Junction ↔ Upper Corridor
- **Elevator (broken):** Gates access to Cryo Bay. Needs repair (parts from Storage Annex). Located off Lower Mid Corridor.

---

## LOWER DECK (13 rooms) — Industrial, rough, where the game begins

### 1. Tug Cockpit
- **Description:** Cramped cockpit of the salvage tug you stowed away in. Instruments dark. Smells like recycled air and regret.
- **Objects:** Flashlight (limited battery), ration bar
- **Exits:** South to Airlock
- **Notes:** Player starts here. After the tug detaches, trying to go back here could be interesting (viewport showing empty space where the docking arm was).

### 2. Airlock
- **Description:** Dim red emergency lighting. A faded corporate biohazard notice on the wall. Claw marks along the inner door frame. Silent.
- **Objects:** Biohazard warning sign (examinable, first lore), emergency locker (empty or near-empty)
- **Exits:** North to Tug Cockpit (until it detaches), south to Forward Corridor
- **Notes:** First room on the Stillwater. Sets the tone.

### 3. Forward Corridor
- **Description:** Long corridor stretching aft. Overhead panels hang loose. Floor plates rattle underfoot.
- **Objects:** Loose floor panel (examine reveals cable conduit below)
- **Exits:** North to Airlock, south to Cargo Bay, east to Maintenance Shaft (cable conduit)

### 4. Maintenance Shaft (Cable Conduit)
- **Description:** Narrow conduit packed with bundled cables and fiber optic lines. The AI's physical nervous system runs through here. Some cables pulse with faint light.
- **Objects:** AI cables (examinable — can be cut in override ending path), junction box
- **Exits:** West to Forward Corridor, south to Engineering
- **Notes:** Shortcut bypassing cargo/cryo. AI cables here are key to the override ending (physical disconnection path). Dark — needs flashlight.

### 5. Cargo Bay
- **Description:** Cavernous bay filled with shipping containers. Some have been pried open and rearranged into a crude living space. Someone's been here a while.
- **Objects:** Opened containers (searchable), improvised bedding, scavenged food wrappers, makeshift barricade
- **NPC:** The Thawed Prisoner (territory — cunning survivor, protective of cryo prisoners)
- **Exits:** North to Forward Corridor, south to Cargo Hold, east to Storage Annex

### 6. Cargo Hold (Deep)
- **Description:** The inner hold. Sealed bulkhead door with multiple warning labels and a corporate security lock. Whatever they were really shipping is behind this door.
- **Objects:** Security lock (access code puzzle), warning labels (lore — corporate classifications), viewport slit (peek inside?)
- **Exits:** North to Cargo Bay
- **Notes:** Sealed until player finds access. Contains the pathogen source / bioweapon containers. Major Act 3 revelation.

### 7. Storage Annex
- **Description:** Supply room off the cargo bay. Shelves of replacement parts. A locked security crate sits in the corner. Someone has been picking through the shelves.
- **Objects:** Spare parts (elevator repair components, food maker parts), locked weapons crate (corporate security gear — pry bar or code to open), hazmat suit/gear, prisoner's stash (hidden — scavenged supplies)
- **Exits:** West to Cargo Bay

### 8. Lower Mid Corridor
- **Description:** Central corridor junction. A ladder shaft goes up through the ceiling. Directions painted on the walls are half-scraped away.
- **Objects:** Direction signs (lore — ship layout hints), wall panel (examinable)
- **Exits:** North to Forward Corridor area, south to Aft Corridor, east to Elevator (broken — gates Cryo), UP to Mid Deck Central Junction (LADDER)

### 9. Cryo Bay
- **Description:** Clinical ward gone wrong. Rows of cryo pods stretch into the gloom, frosted glass fogged from the inside. Fluid leaks from cracked seals. Some status lights blink green. Others don't blink at all.
- **Objects:** Cryo pods (examinable — see faces inside, some empty, some wrong), leaked fluid (pathogen exposure risk), malfunctioning pod (sparking)
- **Exits:** West to Elevator landing, south to Cryo Control
- **Notes:** Gated behind elevator repair. Hundreds of pods. The scale of the prisoner operation hits here.

### 10. Cryo Control
- **Description:** Small control room overlooking the cryo bay through reinforced glass. Banks of monitors show vital signs. Most are flatlined.
- **Objects:** Pod status terminal (monitor status — alive/dead/empty count), selective thaw controls (can wake individuals — risky), emergency purge switch (kill switch — horrifying), prisoner records terminal (who's in the pods, why, crimes)
- **Exits:** North to Cryo Bay
- **Notes:** Major decision point. Multiple endings touch this room.

### 11. Aft Corridor
- **Description:** The corridor narrows here. The deck plates vibrate faintly. It's getting warmer.
- **Objects:** Temperature gauge (rises over the game as reactor wakes up)
- **Exits:** North to Lower Mid Corridor, south to Engineering

### 12. Engineering
- **Description:** The guts of the ship. Pipes, valves, control panels. The reactor hum is low but building — you can feel it in your teeth. Starts cold and dead, gets hotter and louder as the game progresses.
- **Objects:** Reactor controls (state machine — power routing puzzle), comms relay hardware (physical transmitter component — AI needs this + bridge controls to broadcast), tool rack (maintenance tools — multi-purpose/weapon), power junction (reroute power to different systems)
- **NPC:** The Engineer (found here initially — their domain, helpful at first)
- **Exits:** North to Aft Corridor, east to Reactor Room, north-shortcut from Maintenance Shaft

### 13. Reactor Room
- **Description:** The beating heart of the Stillwater. The reactor core sits behind shielding, pulsing with increasing intensity. Warning indicators climb.
- **Objects:** Reactor core (overload controls for destroy ending), radiation shielding (must manage exposure), emergency shutdown, coolant systems
- **Exits:** West to Engineering
- **Notes:** Destroy ending executes here. Radiation is a hazard — needs hazmat gear or limited exposure time.

## MID DECK (7 rooms) — Mixed aesthetic, two wings off central junction

### 14. Central Junction
- **Description:** A wide junction where the mid deck splits east and west. Corporate wayfinding signs on the walls point to departments. The company logo is everywhere.
- **Objects:** Corporate signage (dark humor — "Meridian Solutions: People First"), directory (lists departments), elevator call button (broken — needs repair from other side)
- **Exits:** West to Lab Corridor, east to Hab Corridor, DOWN to Lower Mid Corridor (LADDER), UP to Upper Corridor (LADDER)

### WEST WING (Science/Medical)

### 15. Lab Corridor
- **Description:** Sterile white corridor gone grimy. Fluorescent panels flicker. A chemical smell hangs in the air.
- **Objects:** Chemical spill (old, dried), safety station (eye wash, gloves — minor supplies)
- **Exits:** East to Central Junction, west to Science Lab, south to Medbay

### 16. Science Lab
- **Description:** Workbenches covered in equipment. Sealed sample containers in a cabinet. A research terminal blinks in standby mode. Some instruments have been smashed.
- **Objects:** Pathogen samples (sealed — examining reveals what was being studied), research terminal (partially corrupted logs — key lore about what the pathogen is and does), broken lab equipment (centrifuge, analyzer — repairable? Possibly relevant to secret merge ending)
- **Exits:** East to Lab Corridor
- **Notes:** Major lore location. Pathogen discovery breadcrumbs here.

### 17. Medbay
- **Description:** Medical facility. Treatment beds with restraints. A quarantine cell in the corner — its door is bent outward from the inside.
- **Objects:** Medical supplies (bandages, stim, anti-infection meds — player survival), patient logs (records of crew showing symptoms — pathogen breadcrumbs), quarantine cell (someone broke OUT — bent door, scratches), medical scanner
- **Exits:** North to Lab Corridor
- **Notes:** Infection treatment possible here. Patient logs are key pathogen discovery source.

### EAST WING (Habitation)

### 18. Hab Corridor
- **Description:** Warmer colors here. This was where people lived. Crew photos on a bulletin board, most curling at the edges.
- **Objects:** Crew photos (examinable — names, faces, humanizes the disaster), bulletin board (shift schedules, notices, one cryptic warning note)
- **Exits:** West to Central Junction, east to Mess Hall, south to Library

### 19. Mess Hall
- **Description:** Long tables bolted to the floor. Trays still out from a meal that never ended. A food fabrication unit sits against the far wall, dark and silent. Corporate motivational posters line the walls.
- **Objects:** Food maker (broken — needs parts from Storage Annex), crew personal effects (mugs with names, carved initials in tables), corporate propaganda posters (dark humor — "Your Productivity is Your Legacy"), hidden stash (under a table or behind a panel — someone hid something)
- **Exits:** West to Hab Corridor

### 20. Library (Ship's Data Archive)
- **Description:** Rows of data terminals. The ship's central archive. Screens glow faintly, waiting for queries. This is where the Stillwater keeps its secrets.
- **Objects:** Archive terminals (query the AI for lore — ship history, cargo manifest, pathogen data, prisoner records), data storage racks (physical media, some damaged), comfortable chair (the one nice thing on this deck)
- **Exits:** North to Hab Corridor
- **Notes:** Primary AI interaction point early game (terminals phase). The AI's lore/unreliable narrator system lives here.

## UPPER DECK (5 rooms) — Small, cramped, crew lived and worked here

### 21. Upper Corridor
- **Description:** Narrow corridor with low ceiling. Pipes and conduits visible overhead. Feels like a submarine.
- **Objects:** Ship schematics on wall (partial map — useful early), intercom panel (AI speaks through this once speakers activate)
- **Exits:** DOWN to Central Junction (LADDER), south to Crew Bunks, east to Captain's Cabin, north to Bridge (LOCKED — needs keycard or security override)

### 22. Crew Bunks
- **Description:** Stacked sleeping pods line both walls. Personal lockers underneath. One bunk shows deep scratches in the wall and dark stains on the mattress.
- **Objects:** EVA suit storage (spacesuits for optional exterior access), evidence of infection (scratched bunk — someone was changing here), stun baton (on security rack), personal lockers (some locked, some open — personal items, minor lore)
- **Exits:** North to Upper Corridor, south to Common Area

### 23. Common Area
- **Description:** A cramped rec space. A viewport looks out into black nothing. Someone has dragged furniture against the east door and piled it high.
- **Objects:** Viewport (see the damaged tug still docked, or empty space), barricade (corp officer's work — must negotiate or find another way), air vent access (duct into maintenance system — alternate route?), recreation stuff (frozen in time — half-finished card game, dead screen)
- **NPC:** Corporate Officer (barricaded here — won't open up easily, trades info for favors)
- **Exits:** North to Crew Bunks, east blocked by barricade

### 24. Captain's Cabin
- **Description:** Modest quarters. More space than crew bunks but not by much. A desk, a bunk, personal effects. The captain lived simply.
- **Objects:** Personal effects (photos, letters — humanizes the captain, backstory), keycard (bridge access — in the desk or hidden), hidden journal (handwritten off-the-record notes — the captain knew something was wrong, key lore), desk drawer (locked? Contains something important)
- **Exits:** West to Upper Corridor

### 25. Bridge
- **Description:** The nerve center. Wraparound console, captain's chair, forward viewport showing stars. Every screen is alive and blinking.
- **Objects:** Navigation console (shows destination — reveals the military buyer), comms array controls (what the AI wants connected to engineering relay — midpoint/endgame trigger), captain's log terminal (final entries — what happened in the last days), security override panel (lock/unlock any door on the ship — powerful late-game tool)
- **Exits:** South to Upper Corridor
- **Notes:** Locked until player finds keycard (Captain's Cabin) or finds alternate access. Major Act 3 location. Comms here + relay in Engineering = AI's broadcast capability. Multiple endings converge here.

## ROOM COUNT SUMMARY
- Lower Deck: 13 rooms (including Tug Cockpit)
- Mid Deck: 7 rooms
- Upper Deck: 5 rooms
- **Total: 25 rooms**

## NPC LOCATIONS
- **Engineer:** Engineering (Lower Deck) — moves as infection progresses
- **Corporate Officer:** Common Area (Upper Deck) — barricaded
- **Thawed Prisoner:** Cargo Bay (Lower Deck) — their territory
- **AI-Compromised Crew:** Finds the player — appears mid-game, wanders on AI's schedule

## KEY GATES
- **Bridge:** Locked — needs keycard from Captain's Cabin
- **Cargo Hold:** Security lock — access code puzzle
- **Cryo Bay:** Elevator repair required — parts from Storage Annex
- **Reactor Room:** Accessible but hazardous — needs hazmat gear for extended stay
