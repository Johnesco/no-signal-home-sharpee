 296  ## THE DOCKING SEQUENCE
 297  ##
 298  ## Four one-way states on `the docking controls` — approach, maneuvered,
 299  ## connected, sealed — plus two optional quality steps recorded on entities of
 300  ## their own: `the thrusters` (cold / burned) and `the pressure readout`
 301  ## (idle / live). Skipping the brake costs you the tug. Skipping the pressure
 302  ## check kills you at the far end of the airlock.
 303  ## ===========================================================================
